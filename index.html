A reliable pair of lenses

In everyday French, aperçu means "to perceive" or "to comprehend." For our clients, we're who you talk to when you need to cut through the fog on private tech and digital infrastructure businesses in emerging markets, to discover value.

We're a collective of researchers, operators, and advisors who've spent years learning how Africa's digital economies actually work. How markets move, how policy shapes opportunity, how founders build and how investors decide.

For Investors

We produce independent research on Africa's private technology markets, including company deep dives, sector landscapes, and valuation frameworks that help you allocate capital with confidence, not hope.

For Founders

We are a strategic advisory that combines investment legibility with hands-on deal support to help you build commercial value, position for the right investors, and navigate fundraising without losing focus on the business.

For Partners

We work with funds, corporates, and institutions seeking structured exposure to African technology. Co-research engagements, deal sourcing support, and strategic intelligence on markets that matter.
