01 / Aperçu

A reliable pair
of lenses.

In everyday French, aperçu means “to perceive” or “to comprehend.” For our clients, we’re who you talk to when you need to cut through the fog on private technology and digital infrastructure businesses in emerging markets, to discover value.

We’re a collective of researchers, operators and advisors who’ve spent years learning how Africa’s digital economies actually work: how markets move, how policy shapes opportunity, how founders build and how investors decide.
